TransUnion (NYSE:TRU) has successfully completed its previously announced acquisition of the mobile division of RealNetworks. This acquisition is expected to augment TransUnion’s communications solutions capabilities with advanced telecom technology that uses artificial intelligence to reduce fraud and enhance customer engagement via real-time text, multi-media messages and phone calls analysis. “ TransUnion remains committed to fostering innovation within the telecommunications sector and enabling secure, trusted interactions between businesses and consumers,” said Mohamed Abdelsadek, Chief Global Solutions Officer, TransUnion.
