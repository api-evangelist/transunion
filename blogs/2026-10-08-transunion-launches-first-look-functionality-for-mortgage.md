TransUnion (NYSE: TRU) today launched TransUnion First Look Functionality for Mortgage , enabling mortgage lenders to review credit insights before purchasing scores. The innovation follows TransUnion's recent extension of 99-cent VantageScore® 4.0 mortgage pricing through 2028, with no charge when lenders pull it alongside a FICO® Score. Combined, these advancements help lenders improve origination efficiency, reduce costs and align credit spending more closely with loan progression.
