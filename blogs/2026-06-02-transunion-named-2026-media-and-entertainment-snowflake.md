TransUnion (NYSE: TRU) has been named 2026 Media and Entertainment Snowflake Product Partner of the Year by Snowflake , the AI Data Cloud Company. The award was presented at Snowflake’s annual user conference, Snowflake Summit 26 , and recognizes the rapid customer adoption of TransUnion’s cloud-native solutions on Snowflake’s platform. Through TransUnion’s Premier Tier partnership with Snowflake , joint customers can resolve and enrich identities without data movement or exposing sensitive data outside their Snowflake environment.
