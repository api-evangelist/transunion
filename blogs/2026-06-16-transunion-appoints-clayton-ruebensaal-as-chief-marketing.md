Clayton Ruebensaal has joined TransUnion (NYSE: TRU) as Chief Marketing and Communications Officer, effective June 15, 2026. In this newly created role, Ruebensaal will lead TransUnion’s Corporate Marketing, Product Marketing and Corporate Affairs and Communications teams, all of which serve an important role in shaping and amplifying the TransUnion story across customers, consumers and a global workforce. He will report to TransUnion President and CEO, Chris Cartwright, and serve on the executive leadership team.
