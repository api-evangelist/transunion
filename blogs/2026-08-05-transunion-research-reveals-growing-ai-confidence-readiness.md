TransUnion (NYSE: TRU) today announced its research that found many organizations remain unprepared to scale AI effectively or clearly measure its business impact despite increasing investments in the capability. TransUnion commissioned United Talent Agency’s (UTA) brand advisory division to survey 100 senior marketing and technology leaders at major U.S. brands.
