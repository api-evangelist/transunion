TransUnion (NYSE: TRU) today announced an enhancement to its mortgage credit report, with the addition of TruVision™ Alternative Credit Attributes (ACA 2.0) from its FactorTrust® Alternative Lending Database to expand lenders’ visibility beyond traditional credit data. The new alternative credit attributes give lenders earlier insight into borrower stability and intent, enabling them to prioritize high-potential applicants earlier in the funnel, streamline workflows and focus resources on loans more likely to convert.
